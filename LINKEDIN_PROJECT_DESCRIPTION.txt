MachineGuard AI | Predictive Maintenance & Smart Recommendations

Built a machine-learning-powered Streamlit application to assess industrial equipment failure risk using operating conditions such as temperature, rotational speed, torque, and tool wear.

Key features:
- Random Forest-based machine failure risk scoring
- Low, medium, and high risk assessment
- Rule-based maintenance inspection recommendations
- Bulk CSV assessment and downloadable results
- Interactive industrial monitoring dashboard

Tech stack: Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas, Streamlit, Plotly
Dataset: UCI AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance (synthetic)

Note: A portfolio demonstration, not a validated industrial safety system.
